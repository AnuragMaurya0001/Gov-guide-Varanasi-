🚗 RTO & Driving Licence

Varanasi has two verified RTO/transport entries rather than ten distinct offices. The app also provides 10+ transport services.

How to create a Driving Licence
1. Open Sarathi/Parivahan → 2. Apply for Learner's Licence → 3. Complete learner requirements/test → 4. Apply for Permanent DL → 5. Book/attend driving test where required → 6. Track issuance. Exact documents, fees and rules can change, so verify on the official portal.
